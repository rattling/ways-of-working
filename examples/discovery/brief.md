# Brightwater app: what we need

*From Sam, owner, Brightwater Heating & Plumbing. Sent as an email, pasted here as-is.*

Hi all,

As discussed, we need to get Brightwater into the 21st century. The big firms all have apps and we're losing out. Here's what I want, roughly in order:

1. **Customers book online.** A portal where they pick a slot, say what's wrong, and pay a deposit. Half our calls are people asking when the engineer is coming.
2. **AI scheduling.** Something that works out which engineer goes where so we fit more jobs in a day. More jobs per day is the number I care about.
3. **Live van map** so the office can see where everyone is.
4. **Job sheets on the phone** instead of the carbon pads. Priya keeps saying we lose them.
5. **Dashboards.** Jobs per engineer, revenue per week, that kind of thing. I want to see the business on one screen.
6. Stock. We never know what's on the vans.

My nephew Jordan has already made a start (it's in the `app` folder) and it looks great, so we're not starting from scratch. He's back at uni in two weeks, so whoever picks it up should get what they need from him before then.

Budget: I'd rather not go over what we spent on the new van, so call it £15k, but if it pays for itself I'm open. I'd like the booking portal live before the winter rush (boilers start failing in October).

We've got 9 engineers, Priya and Dan in the office, and about 120 jobs a week, more in winter. Most customers are older homeowners and a couple of letting agents.

Can we have it by Christmas?

Sam
