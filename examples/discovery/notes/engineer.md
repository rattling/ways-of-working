# Notes: Tom, engineer

*Twenty minutes in the van between jobs. Tom has been at Brightwater eleven years and speaks for "most of the lads", though that's his view.*

**The day**

- WhatsApp list in the morning is fine. "Don't change that."
- Four or five jobs on a normal day, two or three if one's a big install.
- Fills the job sheet at the end of each job, on the bonnet or the customer's kitchen table. Takes five minutes. Usually forgets the start time and makes it up.

**On a phone app**

- Half the jobs are in basements, lofts or airing cupboards. No signal. "If it needs signal, it won't get used."
- Hands are filthy or in gloves. Typing is a no. Tapping is fine. A photo is better than writing.
- The bit that takes longest is copying the boiler's model and serial number off the plate. "Let me take a picture of it."
- The customer signature matters. It's what stops arguments about whether the job was done.
- "If I've got to log in every morning, I'll forget the password by Wednesday."

**On the rest**

- Van tracking: "Not a chance." Some of the older lads would walk. Thinks Sam wants it to see who's taking long lunches.
- Scheduling: Priya does it better than a computer would, because she knows which customers are difficult and who's good with older people.
- Stock: would like to know what's on the van, but would rather someone else did it.

**Jordan's app**

- Jordan showed him the job sheet screen on a laptop. Looked fine. Hasn't tried it on a phone.
