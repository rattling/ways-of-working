# Notes: Priya, office manager

*Half-hour chat in the office, typed up afterwards. Dan (office, part-time) joined for the last ten minutes.*

**How a job goes now**

- Customer phones. Priya or Dan takes it, writes it in the paper diary and adds a row to the shared spreadsheet (job ref, customer, address, job type, engineer).
- About 7am Priya sends each engineer their list on WhatsApp. Changes during the day go the same way.
- Engineer does the job and fills a carbon job sheet: times, parts, what they did, customer signature. Top copy to the customer, bottom copy stays in the pad.
- Pads come back to the office "when they come back". Some engineers drop them in daily, most on Friday, a couple when the pad's full.
- Priya retypes each sheet into the spreadsheet, works out the price, and raises the invoice in the accounts package.

**What hurts**

- "I'm not short of jobs. I'm short of getting paid for them." Invoices go out one to three weeks after the job. Cash flow is tight every winter.
- Retyping takes "most of Monday and half of Tuesday". Handwriting, missing part numbers, no times.
- Sheets go missing: dropped in the van, coffee, left at a customer's. If there's no sheet there's no invoice, because she won't bill without the signature. She reckons one in twenty or twenty-five.
- Parts aren't recorded consistently, so the van stock is a guess. Second-order: it's the invoices that matter.

**Things she was clear about**

- Customers booking themselves: "Please no." They pick the wrong job type, a service when it's a breakdown, and the letting agents would double-book. She's happy to keep taking calls. The calls asking "when's he coming" would stop if customers got a text the day before.
- The accounts package can import invoices from a CSV. She's never used it because nothing produces a clean one.
- Hasn't looked at Jordan's app. "Sam showed me a map on his phone."

**Dan, briefly**

- Would like to see the day's jobs and which are done without phoning round.
- Thinks a dashboard is Sam's thing, not the office's.
